ClawKraft Privacy Policy

Effective: September 27, 2026 · Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to clawkraft.com and its public pages, the ClawKraft early-access waitlist, and ClawKraft services that reference this policy. It explains what information ClawKraft processes, why it is used, who may process it on our behalf, how long it is retained, and what controls are available to users.

2. Early-Access Waitlist Information

When you join the ClawKraft early-access waitlist, we collect the name you choose to provide and your email address. We use that information to record your request for early access, confirm your signup, notify you when access becomes available, provide access instructions, respond to related questions, and operate and secure the waitlist.

We do not sell waitlist information, rent it, trade it, or share it for targeted advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use waitlist information for unrelated advertising or profiling.

3. Other Information ClawKraft May Process

Depending on the features you use, ClawKraft may process account and authentication information; project and repository metadata you explicitly authorize; task, Program, WorkPacket, review, qualification and deployment state; tool and action records needed to operate and audit workflows; usage and cost records; support communications; and product preferences.

Private project source code, files, repository contents, infrastructure details, credentials, and project-specific execution state are used only to provide authorized ClawKraft functionality. Private project content is not placed into a shared shipped-capability catalog merely because a workflow succeeded.

4. Technical Information

Like most online services, hosting and application infrastructure may record standard technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, request metadata, referring page, timestamps, and pages or endpoints accessed. We use this information to operate, diagnose, secure, and maintain the service.

We do not seek sensitive personal information such as government identifiers, financial account credentials, or health information through the early-access waitlist. Please do not submit that information in waitlist fields or support messages.

5. How We Use Information

  • to confirm and manage early-access requests;
  • to notify waitlist members when access becomes available and provide next-step instructions;
  • to authenticate users and operate authorized ClawKraft workflows;
  • to preserve durable work state, review and qualification state, and system integrity;
  • to prevent duplicate, stale, unauthorized, or unsafe execution;
  • to provide support and respond to questions;
  • to measure reliability and service usage; and
  • to detect abuse and secure ClawKraft.

6. Service Providers and Connected Services

ClawKraft uses infrastructure and service providers to operate the service, including hosting, database, authentication, and email-delivery providers. Those providers process information on our behalf to provide their services to us. ClawKraft may also send information to third-party services that you explicitly connect or direct ClawKraft to use.

We do not otherwise disclose personal information to unrelated third parties except where required by law or legal process, or where necessary to protect users, the public, or the integrity of the service.

7. Credentials and Secrets

Raw credentials are handled through protected authorization or secret-broker flows and are not intentionally returned in ordinary task payloads, WorkPackets, chat responses, logs, or public surfaces.

8. Retention

We keep early-access waitlist information while your request is pending and for a reasonable period afterward to complete access follow-up, prevent duplicate entries, respond to requests, and maintain service records. Durable project, audit, acceptance, security, and operational records may be retained while needed to provide the service, preserve project history, satisfy contractual or legal obligations, resolve disputes, or maintain system integrity. Temporary authorization material and transient execution data are retained only for their operational lifetime where possible.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, privacy law may provide rights to access, know, correct, delete, or obtain a portable copy of personal information, and to opt out of certain forms of sale, sharing, targeted advertising, or profiling. ClawKraft does not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

You may request access, correction, deletion, or a portable copy of early-access information by emailing privacy@clawkraft.com with the subject “Privacy Request.” We may need to verify that the request comes from the email address associated with the information.

10. Security

ClawKraft uses authenticated access flows, bounded source authority, protected credential handling, durable currentness and acceptance checks, and fail-closed review and deployment controls. For the public waitlist, writes are handled through a server-side service path rather than exposing database write credentials to the browser. No system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

ClawKraft is a developer and work product and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the early-access waitlist.

12. Visitors Outside the United States

ClawKraft is operated from the United States. If you use the service from another jurisdiction, information may be processed in the United States and in locations where our service providers operate, subject to this policy and applicable law.

13. Changes

If we materially change this policy, we will post the revised version at this URL with an updated date. Where appropriate, we may also notify affected users or waitlist members by email.

14. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: privacy@clawkraft.com. General support is also available through the ClawKraft support page.
